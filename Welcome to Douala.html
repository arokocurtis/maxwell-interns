Beaches. Markets. Culture. Business. Ports. Quarters. Douala has it all.

One of the most important cities in Africa.

Take a moment to explore Douala, Cameroon’s largest city and economic powerhouse. From bustling markets and commercial hubs to vibrant nightlife and cultural institutions, Douala stands at the crossroads of tradition and modernity.

Located on the Gulf of Guinea and straddling the Wouri River, Douala is home to the country's largest port and international airport. As Cameroon’s commercial capital, it offers visitors a taste of authentic Cameroonian life, business opportunities, and a mix of cultures.

Whether you're visiting for business or leisure, Douala promises a vibrant, dynamic experience that reflects the spirit of Cameroon.
