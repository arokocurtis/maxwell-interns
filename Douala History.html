The History of Douala

Douala’s history dates back to the 15th century when it was first settled by the Duala people. It became a center for trade and was later colonized by Germany in the 19th century.

After World War I, the city came under French administration and evolved into a key administrative and commercial hub. Since independence in 1960, Douala has grown rapidly into Cameroon’s largest and most cosmopolitan city.
