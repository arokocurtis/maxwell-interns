BAMENDA DISHES

EGUSSI PUDDING

Egussi Pudding is a savory steamed meal made mainly from ground melon seeds (egussi), mixed with ingredients like crayfish, fish, meat, spices, and oil. It's typically wrapped in banana leaves or cooked in a container using the steaming method.

ACHU

Achu is a yellow-colored, soft, elastic mash made from pounded cocoyams, usually served with Achu soup (also called "yellow soup"). The soup is made with red palm oil, limestone (kanwa), spices, and either meat or fish.

NJAMA NJAMA

Njama Njama" refers to the huckleberry leaves, known for their tender texture and slightly bitter but pleasant taste. It's often sautéed and served with a side of fufu corn (cornmeal) or yam.
