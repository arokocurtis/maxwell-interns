WELCOME TO CAMEROON

Cameroon is a country located in the Central Africa, with Yaounde as capital and Doula as the largest city.
Its official languages are French and English. Cameroon has a population of approximately 27.7million.

The currency used is the Central Africa CFA france(XAF). Cameroon is known for its diverse cultures,
beautiful beaches especially the kribi and limbe beach, and the Mount Cameroon.

Cameroon has many national parks and wildlife reserves, including the Waza national Park and the Dja Faunal Reserve,
which is a UNESCO World Heritage Site

Football(soccer) is the most Popular sport. The national team known
as the "INDOMITABLE LIONS", has won multiple African Cups of Nations
title and participated in several FIFA World.

Quick Facts

  • 📛 Official Name: Republic of Cameroon
