Douala's Notable Sights

Douala is filled with sights that blend tradition and modernity. From museums to street markets, visitors will find something captivating.

  • La Nouvelle Liberté – a unique sculpture symbolizing artistic freedom.
  • Marche Central – the main market where you can find food, textiles, and crafts.
  • Doual'art – a center for contemporary art and exhibitions.
