WELCOME TO THE VILLAGE OF KOM

About Kom

The Kom people, located in the Northwest Region of Cameroon, trace their origins to the Tikar ethnic group.
According to oral tradition, their ancestors migrated from the Tikar Plain in the present-day.This migration was likely prompted by inter-tribal conflicts and the need for new land.
After a long journey southward, they finally settled in the lush highlands of the Boyo Division, where the present-day Kom Kingdom is The final and permanent settlement was established in Laikom, which later became the capital of the kingdom The Kom Kingdom was formally founded in the 17th century under the leadership of Fon Yuh, who is remembered as the unifier of the Kom people. He laid the foundation for the royal institution and established a traditional system of governance that is still influential today.
Over the years, the kingdom grew stronger, with Laikom developing as the political,
spiritual, and cultural heart of Kom. The traditional structure of Kom society is hierarchical. At the top is the Fon (King), regarded as the father of the land and the highest spiritual authority. He governs with the support of a council of elders and noblemen, but most importantly, with the Kwifoyn.
The Kwifoyn is a secret society that acts as both a council and a check on the Fon’s power.
It enforces laws, manages disputes, and ensures that the Fon rules justly. This system of balanced power has helped preserve social order and cultural identity in the kingdom for centuries.

Early days

In the early days, the Kom people migrated from the Tikar Plain in the Adamawa Region of present-day Cameroon.
This migration was driven by a combination of tribal conflicts and the search for fertile land. After traveling southwards, they settled in the northwestern highlands, where they founded what is now known as the Kom Kingdom.

Major Events

In the early days of Kom history, one of the most significant events was the migration from the Tikar Plain in the Adamawa region. The Kom people moved southward due to wars and internal pressure, eventually settling in the northwest highlands of Cameroon.
This migration marked the beginning of Kom as a distinct kingdom.

Quick Facts about Kom Village

  • 📍 Location: NW Region, Cameroon
  • 👥 Population: ~1,412 (2005)
  • 🗣 Language: Mfumte
  • 🌾 Crops: Maize, cassava, plantains
  • 🐐 Livestock: Goats, pigs, chickens
  • 🏫 Schools: 1 preschool, 1 primary
  • 🚱 Water: Limited taps
  • ⚡ Electricity: None (as of 2011)
  • 🛣 Roads: Poor; needs 4×4
  • 🛒 Market: No village market
