The Culture of the Bakweri People

Buea is the ancestral home of the Bakweri people, one of the most historically and culturally rich ethnic groups in Cameroon. Their customs, dances, and language (Mokpwe) form the core identity of the region.

Traditional Attire: During cultural festivals, the Bakweri people wear elaborate traditional clothing made of vibrant fabrics, beads, and headpieces symbolizing status and history.

Music and Dance: Drumming and traditional dances are an important part of celebrations like births, weddings, and funerals. The *Liong* and *Malle* dances are popular expressions of rhythm and storytelling.

Festivals: The people of Buea celebrate *Likané la Mosep*, a harvest festival showcasing dance, food, and ancestral honor. These festivals unite families and villages across generations.

Language & Storytelling: The Mokpwe language is still spoken and passed down. Elders tell folk tales and proverbs that teach moral lessons and preserve community wisdom.
