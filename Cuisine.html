Traditional Cuisine of the Buea People

The Buea region, home to the Bakweri people, is rich in traditional dishes passed down through generations. These meals are not only delicious but also reflect the people’s connection to the land and culture.

Fufu and Eru: This iconic dish features pounded cassava (fufu) served with eru — a leafy green vegetable cooked with palm oil, crayfish, beef, and sometimes snails. It's a delicacy found at every traditional gathering.

Kwacoco and Mbanga Soup: Made from grated cocoyam wrapped in banana leaves, kwacoco is typically served with mbanga soup (palm nut soup) and smoked fish. A hearty meal enjoyed especially during cultural events.

Roasted Fish and Plantains: A favorite street food — fresh fish marinated in spices and roasted, served with sweet fried plantains and spicy pepper sauce.

Drinks: Palm wine (locally known as “matango”) is a popular traditional drink, often served fresh at community gatherings. Corn beer and other local brews are also common.
